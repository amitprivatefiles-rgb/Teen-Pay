# Content Guidelines - Google Compliance

## Golden Rules

### 1. Public vs Private Content

**PUBLIC (Google can see)**
- Landing page
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- About us content
- How it works explanations

**PRIVATE (Behind authentication, noindex)**
- User dashboard
- Task lists
- Earnings/wallet
- Withdrawal system
- Admin panel
- Company dashboard

---

## Language Guidelines

### ✅ USE THESE WORDS (Public Pages)

**Engagement Terms**
- Digital engagement
- Community participation
- Brand interaction
- User engagement
- Authentic participation
- Platform features
- Member benefits
- Community activities

**Trust Terms**
- Secure platform
- Verified users
- Privacy-focused
- Transparent process
- Trust and safety
- Quality assurance

**Neutral Activity Terms**
- Opportunities
- Activities
- Participation
- Feedback
- Interactions
- Contributions
- Engagement campaigns

**Soft References (When needed)**
- "Platform features available to members"
- "Exclusive member benefits"
- "Registered user advantages"
- "Participation incentives" (internal only)

### ❌ NEVER USE THESE WORDS (Public Pages)

**Money/Financial Terms**
- Earn money
- Get paid
- Make money
- Cash
- Payment
- Income
- Revenue
- Salary
- Profit
- Money symbols (₹, $, €, £)

**Reward Terms**
- Rewards
- Prizes
- Bonuses
- Incentives (publicly)
- Cashback
- Payouts

**Task-for-Money Terms**
- Paid tasks
- Micro jobs
- Gig work
- Side hustle
- Extra income
- Daily earnings
- Passive income

**Spam Indicators**
- Free money
- Easy money
- Quick cash
- Work from home (in financial context)
- Unlimited earning
- Get rich

## Page-Specific Content Rules

### Homepage

**Good Examples:**
- "Connect with brands you care about"
- "Participate in meaningful community activities"
- "Help businesses grow through authentic engagement"
- "Join our trusted engagement platform"
- "Build genuine brand relationships"

**Bad Examples:**
- "Earn ₹500 daily by completing tasks"
- "Get paid for reviews"
- "Make money from home"
- "Turn your time into cash"
- "Start earning today"

### About Us

**Good Examples:**
- "We connect businesses with engaged community members"
- "Building authentic digital presence through real interactions"
- "A platform focused on trust and transparency"

**Bad Examples:**
- "We help users earn money online"
- "Get paid for your participation"
- "Generate income from tasks"

### How It Works

**Good Examples:**
- "Browse engagement opportunities from brands"
- "Participate in activities that interest you"
- "Registered members access exclusive platform features"
- "Users contribute feedback and receive platform benefits"

**Bad Examples:**
- "Choose tasks and earn money"
- "Get paid ₹10-₹50 per task"
- "Withdraw your earnings via UPI"
- "Refer friends and earn commissions"

- Focus on platform value

- Brand growth

- [ ] No mention of earning/making money

- [ ] Focuses on engagement/community
- [ ] Uses approved keywords only

## Emergency Response

### If Google Flags Your Content

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Remove flagged content immediately
2. Replace with compliant content
3. Request re-review in Search Console
4. Check all pages for similar violations
5. Update robots.txt if needed
6. Add more noindex tags to private pages

**Prevention:**
1. Regular content audits
2. Team training on guidelines
3. Content approval process
4. Automated keyword checking
5. Regular GSC monitoring

---

## Content Update Process

### When Adding New Features

**Step 1: Classify Feature**
- Is it financial/reward-related? → Keep private (noindex)
- Is it informational? → Can be public (with careful wording)

**Step 2: Write Content**
- Use approved language
- Check against prohibited words
- Focus on value, not money

**Step 3: Technical Implementation**
- Add noindex if needed
- Update robots.txt
- Test meta tags

**Step 4: Review**
- Run through checklist
- Test in incognito mode
- Check Search Console

## Quick Reference Card

**When in doubt, ask yourself:**

1. Would this content appear on a professional SaaS platform?
2. Does it focus on value/service over money?
3. Would a bank or government agency approve this content?
4. Does it build trust or chase quick sign-ups?

**If you're unsure → Default to MORE restrictive positioning**

## Final Reminder

**Your platform's success depends on long-term trust with Google, not short-term keyword optimization. When in doubt, be MORE restrictive about public content, not less.**

The internal platform can have all the features users love (earning, withdrawals, rewards), but the public face MUST be a professional engagement platform.

---

**Questions?**

If you're unsure whether specific content is compliant:
1. Check this guide first
2. Test if it would appear on LinkedIn (if yes, probably safe)
3. Ask: "Would this trigger spam filters?" (if yes, don't use it)
4. Default to engagement/community language
